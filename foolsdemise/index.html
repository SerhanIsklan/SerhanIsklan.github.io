Fool's Demise: Lockpick Escape

You told one joke too many at the royal court, and now you're locked in the dungeon. Your only way out is the lock on your cell door, and on the other side of its barred window stands Mamuk, the court wizard cat, casting spells on the lock to keep you inside. Tap when your pick passes a gold zone; three cracks and it snaps.

A game for Android by Nevermiss Studio, in English and Turkish.
