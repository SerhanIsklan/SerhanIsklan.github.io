Fool's Demise: Lockpick Escape: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026
Developer: Nevermiss Studio ("we")
Contact: nevermissstudio@gmail.com

This policy explains what Fool's Demise: Lockpick Escape (the "game") collects, why, and what you can do about it.

1. What stays on your phone

Your progress stays in the game's own storage on your device and is never sent to us:

  • your best runs and the levels you've reached;
  • coins and tokens;
  • settings, including the language;
  • tutorial progress;
  • how your taps land on average, which the game learns to judge your timing fairly on your phone.

Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

2. The world board (GLOBAL)

When a run ends, the game sends these to our leaderboard server to rank it:

  • your score;
  • the run's length;
  • the level you reached, your hits and perfect hits;
  • how many times the run was continued;
  • the game's version;
  • a random player number and a random code the game created on your phone.

The server adds the date and time.

The game's settings. When the game starts, it asks the same server for its settings (things like how quickly the levels speed up or how often ads come), using the same random player number. We use this to tune the game and to compare versions of its settings; the player number decides which version you get.

Ads you were shown. For each ad the game shows, it sends the server the kind of ad, where in the game it showed (continuing a run, doubling coins, between runs), what Google reports it earned, and which settings version you were on, with the same random player number. This tells us which settings work best. It isn't linked to who you are.

How you play. The game also sends the server what happens as you play, with the same random player number and settings version: when the game is opened, when the practice lock is finished, each time a pick breaks (the level, what broke it, and whether you kept going), each run's end (its levels, score, length and cracks), your answer to KEEP GOING, when Google's review card is asked for or RATE THE GAME is tapped, and when Remove Ads is bought. We use it to see where the game is too hard or too easy and to tune it. It isn't linked to who you are, and it's kept for six months.

Your board name. The server gives you a name such as "HappyOtter10679". You don't choose it, and it isn't linked to your real name, email, phone number or Google account. Your board name and best score are shown to everyone who opens the world board; you see your own name on your row there.

Where it's stored. The server is run by Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union), on our behalf. Like any web service, it handles your device's IP address to answer the request and may keep it briefly in its logs. We don't use IP addresses to identify you.

How long. Your scores stay on the board while the game runs it. The board keeps your best score.

Without a connection. The game plays offline. Runs played offline are kept on your phone and sent the next time you're online.

Deleting. In the game, open Settings and tap DELETE MY SCORES. That removes your board name, scores, ad records and play records from the server at once; your runs saved on your phone stay. You can also email us with your board name, and we'll delete them within 30 days.

3. Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob:

  • videos you can choose to watch, to continue a run or double a run's coins;
  • ads between some runs (the one-time Remove Ads purchase turns these off).

To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address;
  • basic device information (model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads.

Google uses this under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any personalised ads, using Google's consent form. Where the law asks for it, you can change your choice any time in Settings › AD PRIVACY.
  • Your advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

4. Purchases

The one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled by Google Play. We receive only a confirmation that you bought it, never your payment details.

5. What we don't collect

  • no account or sign-up;
  • no email or phone number;
  • no location from your phone (GPS or similar);
  • no contacts, photos, camera or microphone;
  • no data sold to anyone.

6. Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child's scores are on the world board, email us and we'll delete them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws may give you the right to access, correct or delete data about you, or to object to how it's used. Examples are the GDPR in the EU and UK and KVKK in Turkey. Email nevermissstudio@gmail.com and we'll answer within 30 days.

8. Security

Scores travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only we can change the leaderboard data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date. Big changes will also be announced in the game. This policy is always one tap away in the game: Settings › PRIVACY POLICY.
